ABOUT

WHO WE ARE

Faith and medicine, connected through community.

The Adventist Medical Students Network – Philippines (AMSN-PH) is a national network that brings together Adventist medical students from different schools, regions, and stages of training.

AMSN-PH creates opportunities for students to grow spiritually, build meaningful relationships, receive guidance and mentorship, develop as future physicians, and participate in service and mission.

Faith

Keeping Christ at the center of the medical journey.

Fellowship

Building a community that understands both medicine and faith.

Growth

Learning through mentorship, leadership, and shared experience.

Service

Using medicine and compassion to serve people and communities.

Medical training can take us to different campuses, hospitals, cities, and communities. AMSN-PH helps Adventist medical students remain connected beyond institutional and regional boundaries.

03

AMSN + AMEN

FROM MEDICAL SCHOOL TO MEDICAL MINISTRY

Part of a larger Adventist medical community.

AMSN-PH has historically served as the medical-student arm of the Adventist Medical Evangelism Network (AMEN) Philippines, helping students begin building a Christ-centered professional and ministry network even before they become licensed physicians.

This relationship creates continuity—from student fellowship and mentorship, to board preparation, professional growth, medical ministry, and connection with Adventist healthcare professionals after graduation.

MEMBERSHIP

FOR RECOGNIZED AMSN MEMBERS

Membership that connects students to people, programs, and opportunities.

For F.Y. 2026–2027, registered and recognized AMSN members may access network benefits designed for holistic and professional growth.

01

Socio-Spiritual Activities

Eligibility to participate in AMSN-hosted socio-spiritual activities, online and face-to-face.

02

Mentor Circle Access

Access to mentorship and guidance through an assigned board-certified medical doctor.

03

Pre-Board Ready Program

Access to board-subject tutorials and support for Physician Licensure Examination preparation.

04

Network Privileges

Access to current and future resources, programs, opportunities, and initiatives of the AMSN network.

05

Student Directory & Official ID

Verified members may be included in the national student directory and issued an official AMSN identification card.

06

Connection to AMEN

AMSN members who successfully pass the physician licensure boards may be endorsed to the Adventist Medical Evangelism Network (AMEN).

Privacy & confidentiality

Membership information is intended for legitimate organizational use such as verification, directory/ID creation, official communications, and event planning. Access should remain limited to authorized personnel in accordance with current AMSN-PH policies and applicable privacy requirements.

LATEST FROM AMSN-PH

8th National Executive Board Turn-Over & Dedicatory Service

THEMEPhysician in the 11th Hour
DATEJuly 24, 2026
TIME7:00–8:00 PM PHT
FORMATOnline • Google Meet

AMSN-PH formally welcomed and dedicated its 8th National Executive Board in an evening of worship, recognition, transition, and commitment to service. The program also honored the faithful work of the outgoing 7th Executive Board as the next group of national leaders accepted the responsibility of carrying the ministry forward.

Centered on the theme Physician in the 11th Hour, the gathering included a devotional message by Pastor Jerry Karundeng, recognition of the 7th Executive Board, introduction of the 8th Executive Board, a charge and oath to the new officers, dedicatory prayer, and the inaugural address of incoming National President Gherneil Dalanon.

OUR STORY

A GROWING NATIONAL NETWORK

From regional communities to a nationwide student ministry.

2017
The beginning

AMSN-PH was founded with three regional member organizations: LSAMS, CAMSA, and ISAMS.

2018
The network expands

AMiCUS–University of Northern Philippines joined, widening the network’s reach.

2020
Service during crisis

The Good Samaritan Project emerged during the pandemic and supported hospitals, churches, calamity victims, and partner organizations.

2021–2023
Growth, prayer, mission, and service

The network continued expanding its chapters, spiritual programs, board support, medical-mission advocacy, publications, and fundraising initiatives.

2026
The 8th National Executive Board

A new national leadership begins its term with renewed focus on networking, mentorship, member support, service, and organizational continuity.
