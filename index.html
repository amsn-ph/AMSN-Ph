ABOUT

WHO WE ARE

A community for Adventist medical students across the Philippines.

The Adventist Medical Students Network – Philippines (AMSN-PH) connects Adventist medical students from different schools, regions, and stages of training in a shared commitment to Christ-centered formation, fellowship, service, and mission.

Through national and chapter-based initiatives, AMSN-PH creates spaces for students to worship together, encourage one another, grow as future physicians, and use medicine as an avenue for compassionate service and witness.

OUR STORY

2017 — PRESENT

Connected by faith. Growing through service. Continuing the mission.

AMSN-PH began when Adventist medical students from different parts of the Philippines sought to build a national community grounded in shared faith, fellowship, and service.

Through the years, the Network has brought students together through prayer, worship, mentorship, medical missions, professional development, publications, board-exam support, and other initiatives that strengthen both faith and calling.
