About AMSN-PH

A national community for Adventist medical students.

The Adventist Medical Students Network – Philippines brings together Adventist medical students from different schools, cities, and stages of training in a shared commitment to faith, fellowship, service, and mission.

AMSN-PH exists to help future physicians remain grounded in Christ, connected with one another, and equipped to serve wherever they are called.

“Medicine is not only a profession we are preparing for. It is also a place where faith can be lived, shared, and put into service.”

Our Story

2017—Today

Connected by faith. Growing through service.

AMSN-PH began as Adventist medical students from different parts of the Philippines sought to build a national community grounded in shared faith, fellowship, and service.

The Network has since brought students together through worship, prayer, mentorship, medical missions, professional development, publications, and other initiatives that strengthen both faith and calling.
