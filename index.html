Who We Are

Faith and medicine. One mission. One network.

The Adventist Medical Students Network – Philippines (AMSN-PH) brings together Adventist medical students from across the country in a shared commitment to Christ-centered medical education, fellowship, service, and mission.

Across different medical schools, cities, and stages of training, AMSN-PH creates a space where future physicians can encourage one another, grow spiritually, develop professionally, and discover meaningful opportunities to serve.

Our calling extends beyond becoming excellent physicians. We seek to become physician-missionaries—health professionals whose knowledge, character, compassion, and service reflect the love of Christ.

Our Story

2017 → Today

Connected by faith. Growing through service. Continuing the mission.

AMSN-PH began as Adventist medical students from different parts of the Philippines sought to build a national community grounded in shared faith, fellowship, and service.

Since then, the Network has brought students together through prayer, worship, mentorship, service, medical missions, professional development, publications, and other initiatives that strengthen both faith and calling.

Today, AMSN-PH continues that story as a growing community of future physicians seeking to remain faithful to Christ while answering the call to heal and serve.
