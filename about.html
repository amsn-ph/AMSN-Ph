About AMSN-PH

A community where faith and medicine grow together.

AMSN-PH brings together medical students and physicians who seek to honor God through learning, service, fellowship, and the practice of medicine.

Mission

Serve faithfully.

To nurture Christ-centered medical students and physicians who support one another, serve communities compassionately, and use medicine as an avenue for mission.

Vision

Grow together.

A connected national network of Adventist medical students and physicians grounded in faith, excellence, service, and lifelong discipleship.

Our values

What guides the network

Christ-centeredness

Faith is not a side activity; it shapes how we learn, serve, lead, and care.

Compassion

We value service that sees the whole person and responds with dignity and kindness.

Community

We build meaningful support across schools, training levels, chapters, and generations.

Excellence

We pursue responsible, ethical, and competent practice as an expression of stewardship.
