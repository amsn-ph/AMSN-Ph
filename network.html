THE AMSN-PH NETWORK

Different schools. Different regions. One shared calling.

AMSN-PH grew by connecting Adventist medical-student communities across the Philippines. This page preserves the documented story of the core organizations that helped build the national network while leaving room for the school-level communities growing today.

Established national network: 2017 Historical core organizations: 5 Current campus expansion: ongoing

HOW THE NETWORK GREW

From three regional communities to a nationwide student network.

AMSN-PH’s 2023 official publication, WITNESS, records that the organization began in November 2017 with LSAMS, CAMSA, and ISAMS. AMiCUS-UNP joined in July 2018, while DSAMS was added during the network’s fourth year, completing a five-organization structure spanning Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

The same archive notes that AMSN-PH was already discovering Adventist medical students beyond these chapter areas, including students from Leyte and Cagayan de Oro. The 2026 direction continues that work through school trustees, representatives, and new campus connections.

01

LUZON

FOUNDING ORGANIZATION • 2017

LSAMS

Luzon Society of Adventist Medical Students

ARCHIVE ROLE Founding regional organization

LSAMS was one of the three regional organizations present when AMSN-PH was founded in November 2017. The official history places AUP-MEDS (Adventist University of the Philippines – Medical Evangelists Dedicated for Service) under the Luzon network.

Its public Facebook identity describes its members as physician missionaries called to bring healing and wholeness to the world—a concise expression of AMSN’s broader integration of medicine and mission.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

The 2023 issue featured the Luzon chapter through physician stories and medical-school reflections, preserving examples of devotional discipline, campus ministry, Sabbath faithfulness, and service alongside academic life.

CEBU

FOUNDING ORGANIZATION • 2017

CAMSA

Cebu Adventist Medical Students Association

ARCHIVE ROLE Founding regional organization

CAMSA helped form AMSN-PH’s original national structure in 2017 and represents the Cebu medical-student community within the historical network.

AMSN-PH’s public chapter feature describes CAMSA through Sabbath faithfulness and a desire to enrich students’ spirituality through religious activities and service. WITNESS also remembers the chapter’s strong enthusiasm for medical missions.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

Dr. Evzel Rosqwyn Baldoza’s CAMSA feature connected medicine with family, music ministry, sports, and medical missions—showing a chapter culture where faith and whole-person development are part of the medical journey.

ILOILO

FOUNDING ORGANIZATION • 2017

ISAMS

Iloilo Society of Adventist Medical Students

ARCHIVE ROLE Founding regional organization

ISAMS is the Iloilo-based society that joined LSAMS and CAMSA as one of AMSN-PH’s three founding regional organizations.

Its public page describes the society as Seventh-day Adventist medical students seeking to become more like the Great Physician. The chapter has also contributed music, prayer, and fundraising support to national activities.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

The 2023 issue featured Dr. Denise Jann Agum, a Central Philippine University graduate, including her experience advocating for Sabbath accommodation during internship—an example of faith lived within a non-Adventist medical environment.

04

ILOCOS

JOINED AMSN-PH • JULY 2018

AMiCUS-UNP

Adventist Ministry to College & University Students — University of Northern Philippines

REGION Northern Luzon

AMiCUS-UNP joined AMSN-PH in July 2018. Its entry widened the national organization’s reach into Northern Luzon and connected the University of Northern Philippines’ Adventist student ministry with the medical-student network.

Unlike the other historical societies, AMiCUS-UNP is a broader campus ministry chapter rather than a medical-only society, while still playing an important role in connecting Adventist medical students at UNP.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

Dr. David Calibuso II’s feature reflects the UNP experience: choosing a medical school compatible with Sabbath convictions, growing from a student into a compassionate future physician, and crediting God, family, mentors, and teachers for the journey.

DAVAO

JOINED DURING AMSN-PH’S 4TH YEAR • 2021

DSAMS

Davao Society of Adventist Medical Students

REGION Mindanao

DSAMS was added during AMSN-PH’s fourth year under the leadership of Marc Philip Ocoma. Its inclusion extended the chapter alliance into Mindanao and completed the five-member-organization structure documented in the 2023 WITNESS history.

The publication also refers to the group in connection with Davao Medical School Foundation, preserving stories of students who supported one another in faith while training in a demanding medical environment.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

Drs. Marie Anthonette Mencidor-Nañez and Jibeth Mudanza were featured together, reflecting on friendship centered on God, spiritual growth, and moments of ministry with patients through prayer and music.

THE NETWORK TODAY

Beyond the original five.

Historical chapters remain an important part of AMSN-PH’s identity, but the network does not need to stop where the old chapter map ended. Adventist medical students may be studying in schools without an established local society or in areas not previously represented.

For 2026–2027, school trustees and campus representatives can help bridge that gap: relaying AMSN-PH information, identifying interested Adventist medical students through privacy-respecting outreach, and helping students participate in national programs while local communities grow.
