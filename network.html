THE AMSN-PH NETWORK

Different schools. Different regions. One shared calling.

We grew by connecting Adventist medical-student communities across the Philippines. Our network story begins with regional organizations and continues today through chapters, school trustees, and campus connections.

Established national network: 2017 Core organizations in our story: 5 Campus connections: growing

HOW THE NETWORK GREW

From three regional communities to a nationwide student network.

Our 2023 WITNESS issue records that AMSN-PH began in November 2017 with LSAMS, CAMSA, and ISAMS. AMiCUS-UNP joined us in July 2018, followed by DSAMS during our fourth year, extending our community across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Our story has always extended beyond formal chapter lines. We continue meeting Adventist medical students in other schools and regions through school trustees, representatives, and new campus connections.

LUZON

FOUNDING ORGANIZATION • 2017

LSAMS

Luzon Society of Adventist Medical Students

ARCHIVE ROLE Founding regional organization

LSAMS was one of the three regional organizations with us when AMSN-PH began in November 2017. Our history places AUP-MEDS (Adventist University of the Philippines – Medical Evangelists Dedicated for Service) within the Luzon network.

The community embraces the calling of physician missionaries who bring healing and wholeness—an expression of how we unite medicine and mission.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

The 2023 issue featured the Luzon chapter through physician stories and medical-school reflections, preserving examples of devotional discipline, campus ministry, Sabbath faithfulness, and service alongside academic life.

ILOILO

FOUNDING ORGANIZATION • 2017

ISAMS

Iloilo Society of Adventist Medical Students

ARCHIVE ROLE Founding regional organization

ISAMS is our Iloilo-based society and joined LSAMS and CAMSA as one of AMSN-PH’s three founding regional organizations.

The society seeks to become more like the Great Physician and has contributed music, prayer, fellowship, and fundraising support to our national activities.

WITNESS ARCHIVE

The 2023 issue featured Dr. Denise Jann Agum, a Central Philippine University graduate, including her experience advocating for Sabbath accommodation during internship—an example of faith lived within a non-Adventist medical environment.

AMiCUS-UNP

Adventist Ministry to College & University Students — University of Northern Philippines

REGION Northern Luzon

AMiCUS-UNP joined us in July 2018, widening our reach into Northern Luzon and connecting the University of Northern Philippines’ Adventist student ministry with our medical-student network.

AMiCUS-UNP is a broader campus ministry rather than a medical-only society, while playing an important role in connecting Adventist medical students at UNP with us.

THE NETWORK TODAY

Beyond the original five.

Our established chapters remain an important part of who we are, but our network does not stop at old chapter boundaries. Adventist medical students may be studying in schools without an established local society or in areas not previously represented.

Through school trustees and campus representatives, we can bridge that gap, share AMSN-PH opportunities, welcome interested students through privacy-respecting outreach, and help new local communities grow.
