01

MEMBERSHIP

FOR RECOGNIZED AMSN-PH MEMBERS

Benefits designed to support the whole medical journey.

For F.Y. 2026–2027, our registered and recognized members have access to benefits designed to support spiritual, personal, and professional growth.

01

Socio-Spiritual Activities

Eligibility to participate in AMSN-PH-hosted socio-spiritual activities, online and face-to-face.

02

Mentor Circle Access

Access to mentorship and guidance through an assigned board-certified medical doctor.

03

Pre-Board Ready Program

Access to board-subject tutorials and support for Physician Licensure Examination preparation.

04

Network Privileges

Access to current and future resources, programs, opportunities, and initiatives of the AMSN-PH network.

05

Student Directory & Official ID

Verified members may be included in the national student directory and issued an official AMSN-PH identification card.

06

Connection to AMEN

Members who successfully pass the physician licensure boards may be endorsed to the Adventist Medical Evangelism Network (AMEN).

Privacy & confidentiality

Membership information is used only for legitimate organizational purposes, including verification, directory and ID preparation, official communication, and event coordination.

Continuing the journey

As members continue through graduation and licensure, AMSN-PH helps nurture relationships, mission, and professional growth that may extend into the wider Adventist medical community, including AMEN.
